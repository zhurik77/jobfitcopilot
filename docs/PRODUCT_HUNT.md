## Product Info

**Tagline (headline):**
> Open-source AI Chrome extension for ATS resume analysis and vacancy fit scoring

**Description (subtitle):**
> Job Fit Copilot analyzes your resume against any job description using AI (OpenAI, Claude, DeepSeek). Get a detailed fit score, ATS compliance audit, keyword gap analysis, and tailored cover letters — all inside your browser. Works on hh.ru, LinkedIn, and Upwork.

**Category:** Developer Tools > Chrome Extensions

**Website:** https://github.com/zhurik77/jobfitcopilot

## First Comment

Post this as the first comment on launch day:

> Hey Product Hunt! 👋
>
> I built Job Fit Copilot because I was spending hours manually comparing resumes to job descriptions — and I knew AI could do it better.
>
> The extension runs entirely in your browser (no data leaves your machine except to the LLM provider you choose). It's fully open source (MIT license) with 555+ tests.
>
> **What makes it different:**
> • 🆓 Free & open source — no subscriptions, no data selling
> • 🤖 Multiple AI providers — OpenAI, Claude, DeepSeek, NVIDIA NIM
> • 📊 ATS audit — see what recruiters' systems see
> • 🔄 Multi-resume comparison — pick your best version
> • 📝 Auto cover letter generator
> • 🚀 Autopilot mode for bulk applications
>
> **Tech stack:** Chrome Manifest V3, Node.js, 555+ tests, CI across Linux/Windows/macOS
>
> We're also looking for **HR experts** to help calibrate our scoring — check out the HR Expert Program in the repo!
>
> Would love your feedback and a ⭐ on GitHub if you find it useful!
