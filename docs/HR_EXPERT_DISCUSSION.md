## 🤝 HR Expert Program — We Need Your Expertise!

**Job Fit Copilot** is an open-source Chrome extension that uses AI (OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepSeek, NVIDIA) to analyze resumes and score their fit against job vacancies.

We're looking for **HR professionals and recruiters** to help calibrate our AI scoring system.

### Why HR experts?

AI scoring is fast and consistent, but it can:
- Overvalue formal keyword matches
- Miss implicit context from adjacent industries
- Misweight soft skills vs hard skills
- Miss cultural fit signals

Your expert judgment makes the AI better for everyone.

### What you'll do
- **1–2 reviews per month** (20–40 min each)
- Compare AI-generated ATS scores with your expert assessment
- Tell us what factors actually matter in real hiring decisions
- Help shape the scoring algorithm

### What you get
- ⭐ **Early access** to new features before public release
- ✍️ **Co-authorship** in publications and research
- 🔗 **LinkedIn recommendation** from the project
- 📈 **Influence** on an open-source product used by your colleagues
- 🎯 **Shape the future** of AI-assisted recruiting
